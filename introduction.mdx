Most experimental apps never find the small group of people who would have loved them. FirstFifty exists to fix exactly that: it connects makers of experimental apps with their first believers, and it makes being early **count**, with a numbered seat you keep forever, a front-row view of the build, and recognition that follows you across the platform.

## What FirstFifty is

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="A launch platform for experimental apps" icon="rocket">
    Makers publish an app page (story, roadmap, devlog, community) in minutes. The page is the app's home while it grows from concept to launched product.
  </Card>

  <Card title="A seat you can point to" icon="ticket">
    Each app has 50 Founding seats and 500 Early seats. Seats are numbered and permanent: Founding #3 is Founding #3 forever, on your profile and on chain if you mint it.
  </Card>

  <Card title="A build-in-public toolkit" icon="satellite-dish">
    Devlogs with per-tier visibility, member polls, milestones, a forum, and structured feedback the maker can adopt and tip you for.
  </Card>

  <Card title="An optional token launch" icon="coins">
    Apps that want one launch a token on Monad through an open curve. Founder tokens vest in a contract and liquidity locks at graduation, enforced by code, not promises.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The idea in one paragraph

Being early is valuable, and today that value evaporates: you told your friends about the app, and nothing remembers that you did. On FirstFifty, backing an app early gives you a **numbered seat** in a scarce tier, **Contribution Points** for the ways you actually help, a place on the **leaderboards**, and, when you want it on chain, a **soulbound backer proof** minted to your wallet that records precisely how early you were. Makers get what every launch actually needs: fifty people who care, visible from day one.
