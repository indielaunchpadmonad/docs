Most indie apps die in obscurity not because they're bad, but because the maker had no way to find the small group of people who would have loved it and shown up for it. FirstFifty fixes that. It's a launch platform that connects indie app makers with early adopters through structured community tiers, transparent devlogs, and an optional fair-launch token on Monad. Makers get a community that's aligned from day one; early adopters get a curated feed of real projects and the receipts to prove they were early. No hype cycles, no rugs just builders and believers in the same place.

## For Makers

FirstFifty gives you the community infrastructure that a launch actually needs, without requiring you to stitch together five different tools:

- **App page** — publish your story, milestones, and tier perks in under 30 minutes; the page lives at a permanent, shareable URL.
- **Devlog** — post updates with per-tier visibility (Public, Members, or Founding 50 only) so your earliest believers get the inside view first.
- **Tiers** — 50 Founding slots and 500 Early slots per app, each a permanent on-profile receipt of being first; slot holders vote on your roadmap and earn Contribution Points.
- **Optional token launch** — open bonding curve on Monad, founder allocation 0–20% with a cliff and vest you set, liquidity locked at graduation all contract-enforced. Prefer no token? Community-only is a fully supported path.

## For Early Adopters

FirstFifty turns "being early" from a story you tell at dinner into a verifiable, compounding record of discovery:

- **Curated discovery feed** — a short, high-signal feed of indie apps worth being early to; no endless scroll through noise.
- **Founding 50 & Early 500 slots** — scarce, numbered tier slots per app that carry real perks: office hours with the founder, roadmap votes, and early feature access.
- **Contribution Points (CP)** — earned through reactions, votes, and milestone interactions; CP compounds into leaderboard rank and feeds the retroactive allocation formula.
- **Retroactive token allocation** — if a project you backed launches a token, your status converts into a claim using the formula `tier × CP × time held`; skin in the game without buying in blind.
