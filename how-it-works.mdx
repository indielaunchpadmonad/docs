FirstFifty is designed around the reality of how indie apps actually launch: slowly, publicly, and with a small group of true believers doing most of the early heavy lifting. The platform gives that reality a structure a page that tells your story, a tiered community that has real skin in the game, and an optional token launch with guardrails that make the whole thing trustworthy. Here's how the full journey works, from your first draft to a live community and beyond.

## The four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Publish your app page">
    Build your app page from the FirstFifty dashboard. Write your story, define what you're building and for whom, configure your Founding 50 and Early 500 tier perks, and set your public milestones. The result is a polished, shareable page in about 30 minutes no design skills required. Every page lives at a permanent, evergreen URL that you can share anywhere.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match with early adopters">
    Once your page is live, FirstFifty surfaces it on the curated discovery feed to the people most likely to care. The Founding 50 and Early 500 tiers are scarce by design 50 and 500 slots respectively which creates natural urgency. Slot holders claim a numbered position on your app that lives on their profile permanently, so they're motivated to join early and stay engaged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build in public together">
    Post devlog updates with per-tier visibility so your Founding 50 gets the inside view first, your Early 500 follows, and the public sees the highlights. Run member polls to co-shape the roadmap, track progress against milestones your community can see, and collect feedback through structured reactions and replies. Your community earns Contribution Points for every meaningful interaction, which keeps engagement genuine rather than performative.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch how you want">
    When you're ready to take the next step, you have two equally valid paths. Launch a token on Monad via an open bonding curve with founder allocation up to 20% on a cliff and vest you configure, and liquidity locked at graduation, all enforced at the contract level. Or skip the token entirely and continue as a community-only project. Both paths are first-class on FirstFifty.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tier structure

Every app on FirstFifty has three membership levels. The tiers are cumulative higher tiers include everything below them.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Founding 50" icon="crown" href="/adopters/tiers">
    **50 slots per app.** The most exclusive tier. A mono-typed slot number on your profile and the app page, founder office hours every month, a vote on the roadmap, and the highest retroactive allocation weight (tier × CP × time held).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Early 500" icon="badge-check" href="/adopters/tiers">
    **500 slots per app.** Public badge with your slot number. Early feature access one week ahead of public, roadmap vote, and retroactive allocation (smaller slice than Founding 50).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Public" icon="globe" href="/adopters/tiers">
    **Unlimited.** Watch devlog and milestone updates, participate in public polls, and buy tokens if the app launches one. No slot required just follow the project.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Token optional

A token launch is never required on FirstFifty. If you choose to launch one, the mechanics are built to be trustworthy by default:

- **Open bonding curve on Monad** — first-party contracts, not a wrapper around a third-party launchpad.
- **Founder allocation** — 0 to 20%, with a cliff and vesting schedule you set; the clock starts at graduation, not at mint.
- **Locked liquidity** — automatically locked when the bonding curve graduates; contract-enforced, not just promised.
- **No-token option** — community-only projects are fully supported and appear in the discovery feed the same as token-backed apps.

<Info>
  Retroactive allocation for tier holders is calculated using the formula **tier × CP × time held**, regardless of whether the app ever launches a token. If a token does launch, that formula determines each holder's claim at graduation.
</Info>
