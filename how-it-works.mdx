FirstFifty is two journeys that meet on the same app page: a maker shipping an experimental app, and the first believers who back it. Here is the whole loop.

## The maker's journey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Draft the app page">
    Name, one-liner, story, category, stage, links. The website link matters most - it becomes the **Try it** button visitors see first. A draft is private until you publish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Publishing makes the page live at a permanent URL and opens the three tiers: **Founding 50**, **Early 500**, and **Public** (unlimited). Your app now appears in Discover.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build in public">
    Post devlogs (public, members-only, or Founding-only), run polls, track milestones, and answer feedback. Your seat holders see the inside of the build.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch a token - if you want one">
    Entirely optional. An open curve on Monad, founder allocation vesting in a contract, liquidity locked at graduation. Community-only apps are a fully supported path.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The adopter's journey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discover">
    Browse the [Discover feed](/platform/discover) - filter by category, stage, or whether the app has a token. Every card shows how many seats are left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim a seat">
    On an app you believe in, back it: seats in capped tiers are numbered in claim order. On token apps, a qualifying buy claims the seat; on community-only apps you join directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show up">
    Vote in polls, give feedback the maker can adopt (and tip you for), react to devlogs. Everything you do earns [Contribution Points](/adopters/contribution-points).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the receipt">
    Your seat lives on your profile forever. Seat holders on token apps can also mint the **soulbound backer proof** - an on-chain ticket recording their seat and how early their wallet bought.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens when a token graduates

A token app's curve has a visible target on its page. When the community fills it, the launch **graduates**: trading moves to a Uniswap pool whose liquidity is locked permanently, the founder's vesting clock starts, and trading continues right on the app page. Judgement about which apps deserve that moment belongs to seat holders and contributors - the people who showed up - not to a scoring machine.
