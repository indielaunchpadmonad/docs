## How it works in four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Publish your app page">
    Write the story, set your tiers, and add milestones.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get approved and discovered">
    The team reviews each app by hand, then it appears in Discover with live seat counts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build in public together">
    Post devlogs with per-tier visibility, run polls, and hit milestones with people who are invested.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch your way">
    Stay community-only, or open a token curve on Monad with founder vesting and locked liquidity enforced by contract.
  </Step>
</Steps>
