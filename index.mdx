FirstFifty is the launch platform built for indie makers who want more than a contract address and a prayer. Publish your app page in minutes, match with early adopters who genuinely want to see you succeed, build in public with a community that's invested from day one, and — if you choose — launch a token on Monad with anti-rug mechanics enforced at the contract level. Whether you're a maker looking for your first fifty believers or an early adopter hunting for the next breakout app before the crowd arrives, FirstFifty is where that connection happens.

## How it works in 4 steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Publish your app page">
    Write your story, configure your Founding 50 and Early 500 tiers, and set your milestones. FirstFifty gives you a polished, shareable app page in about 30 minutes — no design work required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match with early adopters">
    FirstFifty surfaces your app to the people most likely to care. Scarce tier slots create urgency, and the curated discovery feed means your page lands in front of genuinely interested eyes, not random scrollers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build in public together">
    Post devlog updates with per-tier visibility, run member polls, hit milestones, and collect feedback — all inside the platform. Your Founding 50 are invested, so they actually show up and engage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch how you want (or skip the token)">
    When you're ready, open a bonding curve on Monad with contract-enforced founder vesting and locked liquidity at graduation. Or skip the token entirely — community-only is a first-class choice on FirstFifty.
  </Step>
</Steps>
