The leaderboards make being early publicly verifiable. Your rank isn't something you remember or claim in a conversation — it's scored, updated, and visible to anyone on the platform. FirstFifty tracks four distinct boards, each measuring a different dimension of early activity: breadth of backing, depth of belief, momentum of projects, and proximity to a milestone that changes everything. Together they form a live map of who was early, what they backed, and how those bets are playing out.

## How belief score works

The Top Believers board uses a belief score that only becomes meaningful once at least one launch has graduated its bonding curve. That threshold exists by design — belief score isn't based on intent, it's based on outcomes.

Your belief score is the sum of three components:

- **Early buys on graduated tokens** — if you bought early on a bonding curve and that token graduated, those buys register in your score.
- **Held seats on graduated tokens** — if you held a Founding 50 or Early 500 slot through to graduation, that seat is counted in your score. The tier of your seat influences the weight.
- **Contribution Points (CP)** — the CP you earned through engagement with that app compounds into your belief score alongside the buy and seat components.

The score reflects the full picture of your early commitment: not just whether you bought tokens, but whether you showed up consistently before anyone knew the project would graduate.

## Why your rank matters

Your leaderboard rank is a public, permanent record of your early activity on FirstFifty. It doesn't reset, it doesn't expire, and it can't be gamed with late-stage capital. As more apps launch and graduate, the platform's history of who was early and what they backed becomes increasingly legible — and your rank reflects where you stood in that history.

Rank also compounds into your retroactive allocation on future launches. The belief score and CP that feed your leaderboard position are the same variables that determine your claim when a token graduates. Being high on the Top Believers board isn't just a reputation signal — it's a reflection of the same activity that earns you allocation.

See [Contribution Points](/adopters/contribution-points) for a full breakdown of how CP is earned and how it feeds into both your leaderboard rank and your retroactive allocation formula.

<Note>
  Leaderboards fill as people take slots on live apps and launches graduate. The Most Backers board is live from day one. The Top Believers board fills once the first token launch graduates its bonding curve — scores are calculated retroactively from all activity up to that point, so engaging now still counts.
</Note>
