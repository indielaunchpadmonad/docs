Your rank is not something you remember or claim in conversation. It is scored, updated, and visible to anyone on the platform. Four boards each measure a different side of being early.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Most Backers" icon="users">
    Ranked by how many apps you have backed with a claimed seat. Rewards breadth: people who keep finding and backing new apps. Live from day one.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Top Believers" icon="trophy">
    Ranked by belief score, the most prestigious board. It fills once the first launch graduates, and scores count all earlier activity.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Trending" icon="chart-line">
    Apps gaining the most traction right now, from new seat claims and community activity. Use it to see what the platform is paying attention to.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Closest to Graduation" icon="flag-checkered">
    Apps whose curves are nearest to filling. A last window to get in before a token launch graduates.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What feeds belief score

Belief score is built from outcomes, not intent, which is why the board only fills after a launch has graduated.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Early buys on graduated tokens">
    Buying early on a curve that goes on to graduate registers in your score.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Held seats on graduated tokens">
    A Founding or Early seat held through graduation counts, and the seat's tier influences its weight.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contribution Points">
    The [CP](/adopters/contribution-points) you earned on that app compound into the same score.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why rank matters

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="It is public and permanent">
    Rank does not reset or expire, and it cannot be bought with late capital. As more apps launch, the record of who was early and what they backed only gets clearer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It reflects real behavior">
    The same activity that lifts your rank, seats, early buys, and contribution, is what shows makers you are a believer worth listening to.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  The Top Believers board is calculated from all activity up to the first graduation, so engaging now still counts.
</Note>
