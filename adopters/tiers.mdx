Every app on FirstFifty has exactly 50 Founding slots and 500 Early slots. Once those slots fill, they're gone — no exceptions, no extensions, no minting more after the fact. That scarcity is the point. Claiming a slot isn't a subscription tier you pick from a pricing page; it's a timestamped, numbered receipt that you were among the first people to believe in something before it was obvious.

## Tier comparison

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Founding 50" icon="crown" href="/adopters/tiers">
    **50 slots per app**

    The highest tier. Your slot number is mono-typed on your profile and on the app's page — a permanent, visible record of how early you were.

    - Mono-typed slot number on your profile and the app page
    - Founder office hours, monthly
    - Vote on the app's roadmap
    - Retroactive allocation: **tier × CP × time held** (highest multiplier)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Early 500" icon="bolt" href="/adopters/tiers">
    **500 slots per app**

    The second tier. Your numbered badge is public and your slot is recorded on the app's page.

    - Public badge with slot number
    - Early access to new features, one week ahead of public
    - Vote on the app's roadmap
    - Retroactive allocation (smaller slice than Founding 50)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Public" icon="globe" href="/adopters/tiers">
    **Unlimited**

    No slot number, but you can still follow the app's progress and participate in open activity.

    - Watch devlog and milestone updates
    - Vote in public polls
    - Buy tokens if the app launches one
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to claim a slot

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse the discover feed">
    Head to the [discovery feed](/platform/discover) to see a curated list of indie apps currently accepting early backers. The feed surfaces projects at different stages so you can find one worth backing early.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a project you believe in">
    Read the maker's story, review the milestones, and check the devlog. Founding 50 slots go fast — the makers building in public attract early attention quickly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the app's page">
    Click through to the full app page to see how many Founding 50 and Early 500 slots remain. Slot counts are live and accurate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim your tier slot">
    Select the tier you want — Founding 50 or Early 500 — and confirm your claim. Your slot number is assigned immediately in order of claim, and it appears on your profile and the app's page right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Slot scarcity

The 50-slot limit for Founding 50 is not an artificial marketing cap — it's a contract with the people who are actually first. Once all 50 slots on an app are claimed, no more Founding 50 slots exist for that app, ever. There is no waitlist that converts, no way for a maker to add more after traction picks up.

This matters because retroactive allocation is based partly on tier. Founding 50 holders carry the highest multiplier in the formula. If an app launches a token and graduates, every Founding 50 slot holder benefits from having claimed early. Early 500 slots carry a smaller multiplier but are still significantly more valuable than public access — and there are only 500 of them per app.

The scarcity also makes your slot number meaningful on its own. Slot #1 is provably, verifiably earlier than Slot #7. Your profile shows it, the app's page shows it, and it can't be edited or reassigned.

<Note>
  Slot numbers are assigned strictly in order of claim — first come, first numbered. Slot #1 is provably earlier than Slot #7, and that ordering is permanent. If you're backing an app you believe in, claiming sooner gives you a lower number and a higher time-held weight in the retroactive allocation formula.
</Note>
