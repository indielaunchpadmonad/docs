A seat is a numbered, permanent claim on having been early to one app. Every app has the same ladder; your seat number is the order you arrived in.

## The ladder

| Tier | Seats | How it's claimed | What it carries |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Founding** | 50 | Qualifying buy (token apps) or join/grant | Founding-gated devlogs, founding badge, the largest seat CP, earlier seats earn more |
| **Early** | 500 | Qualifying buy at a lower threshold, or join/grant | Members content, early badge, strong CP |
| **Public** | Unlimited | Free join | Full membership: forum, polls, feedback, devlog reactions |

## The seat rules, precisely

These are the rules the platform enforces, worth knowing before you buy:

- **One single buy qualifies.** Each capped tier has a dollar threshold shown on the tier card. A single buy at or above it claims the tier's next open seat. **Split buys don't add up**: the measure is per transaction.
- **Bigger buys take the best open tier.** If your buy clears the Founding threshold and Founding has seats, you're Founding; if it's full, your claim rolls down to the next tier with room.
- **Seats are permanent.** Selling your tokens later does not touch your seat. It records that you were early; it is not a balance.
- **Moving up is real.** Already Public or Early? A later qualifying buy moves you up, with a new seat number in the higher tier, and your Contribution Points top up to the higher seat's rate.
- **Seat number is not the same as buy order.** Your seat number is your place in the tier; your wallet's position in the token's buy order is a separate fact, and the backer proof records both.

## The backer proof

On token apps, seat holders can mint the **FirstFifty Backer Proof**: a soulbound (non-transferable) on-chain ticket in your wallet. It shows your tier and seat as the headline, your wallet's curve buy order on the stub, and it renders as an actual ticket in wallets and marketplaces. It cannot be sold, transferred, or faked, which is the entire point.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hold a seat">
    Any capped-tier or granted seat on a token app qualifies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet">
    The proof is minted to the wallet linked to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mint from the app page">
    The claim card appears on the app's page for eligible seat holders. One transaction; yours forever.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Makers can also grant seats by hand: the tester who was there before the page existed deserves a number too. Granted seats sit in the same ladder and are marked as granted.
</Note>
