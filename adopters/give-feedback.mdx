Structured feedback is one of the most valuable things you can do for a maker. Adopted feedback earns you **50 Contribution Points**, and makers of token apps can tip you.

<Frame caption="The Feedback section on an app page. Every item opens a thread in the forum where the team answers.">
  ![The feedback section of an app page](/images/app-feedback.png)
</Frame>

## File feedback

You need to be signed in, and you cannot file feedback on an app you are on the team of.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app page">
    Scroll to the **Feedback** section on the Overview tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a type">
    Toggle between **Idea**, **Bug**, and **General**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a short summary">
    Up to 140 characters. One line is enough, and it is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the body">
    The box is pre-filled with a template: what you tried, what happened, where (screen or page), how often, what you expected, and what you would do if this never gets fixed. Answering those makes your report actionable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach images if they help">
    Up to four, each up to 5 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Send feedback">
    You will see "Feedback sent." A forum thread opens for it automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After you send it

- Seat holders can **upvote** feedback, so good items rise.
- The maker sets a status. You will see **planned**, **shipped** (adopted), or **declined**, and any public response they write.
- You can delete your own item while it is still open. The forum thread is deleted with it.

## Adopted feedback pays

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="50 Contribution Points" icon="star" href="/adopters/contribution-points">
    Awarded when the maker adopts your feedback. It cannot be undone.
  </Card>

  <Card title="A tip on token apps" icon="coins">
    Makers can tip from the app's contribution pool. You will see a "tipped" badge on the item. If you have no wallet linked yet, the tokens are held for you until you link one.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  The best reports are specific and reproducible. "The timer resets when I switch spaces, every time, on macOS 15" beats "timer buggy".
</Tip>
