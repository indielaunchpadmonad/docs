Contribution Points (CP) are FirstFifty's record of how much you actually help. Holding a seat says you were early; CP says you were useful. They are earned by doing things, never bought, and every award is written to a ledger, so your total always adds up.

## Every way to earn CP

| Action | CP | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claim a capped seat (Founding or Early) | **100 minus your seat number**, never below 10 | Once per seat. Seat #1 pays 99, seat #25 pays 75, seat #90 and beyond pay 10 |
| Claim a Public seat | **5** | Flat rate |
| Bring someone in with your referral link | **20** for you, **10** for them | When they join an app through your link |
| Your referral makes a qualifying buy | **30** | Only within 30 days of the referral link being used |
| Feedback you gave is adopted by the maker | **50** | The maker marks it adopted |
| Vote in a member poll | **2** | Once per poll |
| Be one of the first 50 buyers of a token | **25** | Once per token, by your wallet's buy order |
| Hold through graduation | **100** | You bought early and still hold when the token graduates |

<Info>
  Referral CP has a daily cap of **200 per person per UTC day**. Awards past the cap are skipped, not queued.
</Info>

<Note>
  Devlog reactions and milestone activity do not earn CP today. Only the actions in the table do.
</Note>

## Moving up a tier

If you join as Public and later make a qualifying buy that moves you into Founding or Early, your seat CP **tops up** to what a straight claim of the new seat would have paid. You never lose what you already earned, and you end up level with someone who claimed the higher seat directly.

## A worked example

You claim Founding seat #7, vote in 5 polls, get one piece of feedback adopted, buy in among the first 50 buyers, and hold until the token graduates.

| Award | CP |
| --- | --- |
| Founding seat #7 (100 minus 7) | 93 |
| 5 poll votes (5 times 2) | 10 |
| Adopted feedback | 50 |
| Early buyer | 25 |
| Held through graduation | 100 |
| **Total** | **278** |
