Contribution Points are FirstFifty's record of how much you actually help. Holding a seat says you were early; CP says you were useful. They are earned by doing things, never bought, and they feed your rank on the [leaderboards](/adopters/leaderboards).

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Earned, not bought" icon="hand-holding-heart">
    There is no way to purchase CP or speed it up with money. Showing up is the only route.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Seat-weighted" icon="ticket">
    A better seat earns more per action: earlier Founding seats earn the most, Early seats a strong share, Public members a starting share.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Public and permanent" icon="trophy">
    CP compounds into your rank across every app you back, and your rank does not reset.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Topped up on upgrade" icon="arrow-up-right-dots">
    If a later qualifying buy moves you into a higher tier, your CP tops up to that seat's rate.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How you earn CP

<Steps>
  <Step title="Claim a seat">
    Taking a seat on an app earns CP straight away. The earlier and higher the seat, the more it is worth.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Engage with the build">
    React to devlog entries, vote in member polls, and take part in milestones. Each is a small, real signal that you are paying attention.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give feedback the maker uses">
    Structured feedback that a maker adopts is one of the most valuable things you can do. Makers can also tip contributors whose feedback helped.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stay through the launch">
    Holding your seat through a token launch that graduates is recognized, and early buys on a graduating curve count toward your standing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What CP does for you

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="It sets your leaderboard rank">
    CP is one of the inputs to the Top Believers board, alongside early buys and held seats on graduated tokens. See [Leaderboards](/adopters/leaderboards).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It shows makers who showed up">
    Makers see who reacts, votes, and gives feedback. Consistent contributors are the ones they notice, thank, and tip.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It is part of your public record">
    Your profile shows the seats you hold and the contribution behind them, so being early is something you can point to instead of just remembering.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Info>
  CP is deliberately not a money-for-points scheme. Two people with the same seat can end up far apart on the boards purely through how much they contributed.
</Info>
