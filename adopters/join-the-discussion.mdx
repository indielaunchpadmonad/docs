Every app has a forum where backers and the maker talk. You do not need a seat to post, only an account.

<Frame caption="The Forum tab. Sort by Active, Top, or New.">
  ![An app forum thread](/images/app-forum-threads.png)
</Frame>

## Start a thread

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Forum tab">
    On an app page, choose **Forum** next to Overview. Sort threads by **Active** (most recently replied to), **Top** (highest scoring) or **New**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press New thread">
    If you are signed out you will see "Sign in to post." Once signed in, **New thread** opens the composer. On an empty forum the button reads **Start the first thread**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write it">
    **Title** is required, up to 200 characters. The body is a Markdown editor with a **Preview** tab, up to 20,000 characters, and an **Image** button for PNG, JPEG or WebP files up to 5 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Post thread">
    Your thread appears with your name and your badges, such as your tier.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reply and vote

- Press **Add a comment** under a thread and choose **Comment**, or press **Reply** on any comment to nest under it.
- Upvote and downvote threads with the arrows beside them.
- You can delete your own threads and comments. The team can moderate and can **Pin** a thread, which shows "Pinned by the team".
- Every thread has a permalink, so you can share it with **Share**.

## Take part in the rest of the app

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Vote in a poll">
    Seat holders click an option on the app page and see "Vote counted". You can tap a different option to change your vote. Each vote earns 2 Contribution Points.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Read gated devlogs">
    Devlog entries for backers show a lock and "{Tier} backers and up can read this." Hold that tier or a more exclusive one to read the body.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Watch milestones">
    The maker's milestones are public on the app page. You are notified when one is hit.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  Forum threads and comments do not earn Contribution Points on their own. Polls, adopted feedback and seats do. See [Contribution Points](/adopters/contribution-points) for the full list.
</Note>
