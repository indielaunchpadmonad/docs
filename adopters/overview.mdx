Being early on FirstFifty stops being a story you tell and becomes a receipt. When you claim a slot on an indie app — before it has users, before it has traction, before anyone else knew to look — that slot number is permanently yours. It lives on your profile, it lives on the app's page, and it compounds into a leaderboard rank and retroactive token allocation that reflects exactly how early and how committed you were. FirstFifty is built for the people who find things first and want proof to show for it.

## Real perks, not a Discord role

Being a Founding 50 or Early 500 member comes with tangible benefits that go well beyond a colored username:

- **Curated discovery feed** — every app in the feed has been selected because it's worth your attention, not because it paid to be there.
- **Numbered slot receipt** — your Founding 50 or Early 500 slot number is permanently attached to your profile and the app's page, proving exactly when you showed up.
- **Founder office hours** — Founding 50 members get monthly direct access to the maker, not just a public Discord channel.
- **Roadmap vote** — both Founding 50 and Early 500 members influence where the app goes next through structured roadmap polls.
- **Retroactive token allocation** — if the app launches a token and graduates its bonding curve, your early backer status becomes a claim. No purchase required to earn it.

## Retroactive allocation

If an app you backed decides to launch a token on Monad and that token graduates its bonding curve, your slot doesn't just stay a badge — it becomes a claim. The allocation formula is:

**tier × CP × time held**

Your tier multiplier is highest for Founding 50, lower for Early 500. Your Contribution Points reflect how actively you engaged — reactions, votes, milestone interactions. Time held rewards patience: the longer you've held your slot, the more weight it carries. No purchase is required to earn retroactive allocation. The system is designed to reward genuine early believers, not last-minute speculators.
