You find good things early. FirstFifty makes that a record instead of a story: every app you back gives you a **numbered seat**, everything you do to help earns **Contribution Points**, and - on token apps - the whole thing can be minted to your wallet as a **soulbound backer proof**.

## What you collect

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Numbered seats" icon="ticket" href="/adopters/tiers">
    Founding 50 and Early 500 seats are claimed in order and kept forever. "Founding #3" means exactly what it says, on your profile and on the app's backers page.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Contribution Points" icon="star" href="/adopters/contribution-points">
    Earned for the ways you actually help: claiming early seats, feedback the maker adopts, votes, referrals, buying early, holding through graduation.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Leaderboard rank" icon="ranking-star" href="/adopters/leaderboards">
    Boards for the most-backed adopters and the top believers - plus daily badges for the top hundred.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Soulbound backer proof" icon="shield" href="/adopters/tiers#the-backer-proof">
    On token apps, seat holders mint a non-transferable on-chain ticket: tier, seat number, and their wallet's buy order on the curve. Yours forever, by construction.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## And the tangible stuff

Being seated isn't only recognition:

- **Gated devlogs** - Founding and members-only posts show you the inside of the build.
- **Polls** - makers put real roadmap choices to their members.
- **Tips** - when a maker adopts your feedback on a token app, they tip you from the app's contribution pool. No wallet yet? The tip waits for you.
- **Perks the maker sets** - office hours, early features, whatever they've promised their fifty.

## Start in two minutes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">

  </Step>
  <Step title="Find an app">

  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim your seat">
    Join directly on community-only apps; a qualifying buy claims it on token apps.
  </Step>
</Steps>
