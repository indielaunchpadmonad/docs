A seat is your numbered place among an app's first backers. This walks you through claiming one.

## Find an app

Browse [Discover](https://firstfifty.xyz/discover) and open an app you like. Cards show how many seats are left.

  ![The Discover feed](/images/discover.png)
</Frame>

## Read the tier card

On the app page, the line under the title reads like "50 of 50 Founding seats left, you'd be Founding #1". The **Tiers** card on the right lists each tier.

<Frame caption="The top of an app page: seats left and the number you would get.">
  ![The top of an app page with a token](/images/app-hero-token.png)
</Frame>

<Frame caption="The Tiers card. Founding and Early each need one buy of the amount shown; Public is free.">
  ![The Tiers card](/images/app-tiers.png)
</Frame>

| Tier | Seats | How you get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Founding** | 50 | One buy of the token at or above the amount on the card (by default $69) |
| **Early** | 500 | One buy at or above the amount on the card (by default $10) |
| **Public** | Unlimited | Free. Press **Join** |

<Note>
  A single buy has to reach the threshold. Two smaller buys do not add up. Your seat stays yours even if you sell every token.
</Note>

## Claim your seat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Press **Back the app**. If you are signed out you are sent to sign in and brought straight back with the dialog open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a tier">
    The **Back** dialog (titled with the app name) lists each tier with its seats left and what it needs. You will see which number you would get. Tiers you already outrank say "below your seat".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join Public for free, or buy in">
    **Join** on the Public tier needs no wallet: you will see "You're in. Public #1." For Founding or Early you make one buy, and this needs a wallet linked to your account and connected. The button tells you what is missing: "Link a wallet to buy", "Connect your linked wallet", or "Switch to Monad".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the buy">
    The popup is titled "Take a Founding seat" and includes a small allowance for price moves. When you confirm, your seat lands once the trade is indexed, usually within a minute. If the tier fills up first, you are placed in the next tier with room.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The Back the app dialog lists each tier and what it needs.">
  ![The Back the app dialog](/images/adopt-dialog.png)
</Frame>

## After you have a seat

<Frame caption="After joining Public: You're in Public #1 and a Move up a tier button.">
  ![The app hero after joining Public](/images/adopt-joined.png)
</Frame>

The hero now says "You're Public #1". A card appears that you can share to X or Warpcast, or download. If you hold a seat on a token app and have a linked wallet, a **Backer proof** card appears on the app page: press **Claim your Founding #1 SBT** to mint the non-transferable on-chain ticket.

Already Public or Early? Press **Move up a tier**. A later qualifying buy gives you a new seat number in the higher tier, and your Contribution Points top up to match.
