Every published app opens the same three tiers. The caps are the product: a numbered seat in a capped tier is scarce enough to want and permanent enough to keep.

## How a seat is claimed

**On community-only apps:** joining claims a seat directly, in claim order.

**On token apps:** a capped seat is claimed by a **single qualifying buy** of the app's token. The rules, exactly as the platform applies them:

- **One buy qualifies.** The tier thresholds are dollar values shown on the tier card (Founding higher, Early lower). A single buy at or above a threshold claims that tier's next seat.
- **Split buys don't add up.** Two half-sized buys claim nothing; the measure is per transaction.
- **Bigger buys take the best open tier.** A large buy claims Founding while seats remain; when a tier is full, the claim rolls down to the next tier with room.
- **The seat survives selling.** Once minted, the seat is yours even if you sell every token. It records that you were early - it is not a balance.

## Granting seats by hand

Some of your first fifty aren't buyers - they're the tester who's been with you since the prototype. From your dashboard you can **grant a seat** to any account by handle, or hold one against an email address that hasn't signed up yet. Granted seats are numbered like any other and marked as granted in the record.

## Perks that are built in

- **Gated devlogs** per tier
- **Badges** on the backer's profile (founding member, early member)
- **Contribution Points** on claim - earlier seats earn more
- **The soulbound backer proof** (token apps): seat holders can mint an on-chain ticket showing their tier, seat number, and how early their wallet bought

<Note>
  Team members can't hold seats on their own app - seats are for backers, and the platform enforces that.
</Note>
