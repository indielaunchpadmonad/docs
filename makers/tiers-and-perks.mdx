Every app on FirstFifty automatically comes with three tiers: Founding 50, Early 500, and Public. You don't set up the tier structure — it's built into the platform. What you configure is the perks attached to each tier: what Founding 50 members get that Early 500 members don't, and what both get that public visitors don't. The scarcity is the mechanic. Fifty Founding 50 slots, five hundred Early 500 slots, globally, ever. Once they're claimed, they're gone.

## Slot receipts

When someone claims a Founding 50 or Early 500 slot on your app, they receive a permanent on-chain receipt tied to their slot number. The receipt is timestamped at claim time and cannot be transferred or duplicated. Slot `#3` will always be slot `#3` — it's a verifiable record that this person was among the first to back your app.

This matters for two reasons. First, it makes early support legible: a Founding 50 badge isn't a follower count or a like — it's a provable claim with a number attached. Second, it creates a social incentive to claim early: lower slot numbers carry more status, and the retroactive allocation formula rewards time held, so getting in at slot `#3` versus slot `#47` has a compounding effect.

## Contribution Points (CP)

Contribution Points accumulate when backers interact with your app: reacting to devlog entries, voting on polls, completing milestone interactions, and participating in roadmap votes. CP is tracked per backer, per app. It reflects how engaged someone is with your build over time — not just that they claimed a slot, but that they kept showing up.

CP compounds into leaderboard rank and into the retroactive allocation formula. For detailed mechanics on how CP is earned and tracked, see the [Contribution Points guide](/adopters/contribution-points).

## Retroactive allocation formula

If your app launches a token and reaches graduation, backers with tier slots become eligible for a retroactive allocation. The formula is:

```
retroactive allocation = tier weight × CP × time held
```

- **Tier weight** — Founding 50 carries a higher multiplier than Early 500. Public backers are not eligible.
- **CP** — total Contribution Points earned on your app. More engagement means a larger slice.
- **Time held** — how long the backer has held their slot. Claiming early and staying engaged maximizes the return.

The formula rewards the backers who were most invested — both financially (via tier) and behaviorally (via CP and time). It aligns your community's incentives with your app's long-term success.

<Note>
  You don't need a token to use tiers. Community-only mode is fully supported — Founding 50 and Early 500 tiers, devlog visibility controls, polls, milestones, and Contribution Points all work without any token attached to your app. Retroactive allocation simply doesn't apply if there's no token to graduate.
</Note>
