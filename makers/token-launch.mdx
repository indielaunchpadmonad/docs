A token is optional on FirstFifty - and when you choose one, the safeguards are **contract-enforced, not promised**. Everything below is what the deployed contracts actually do; the numbers your launch uses are shown on the launch screen and on your app page.

## The launch

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure">
    Token name and ticker. Supply is fixed for every launch (no supply games between apps). Choose your founder allocation - anywhere from **0% up to 20%** - and its vesting schedule within the platform's floors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch the curve">
    One transaction deploys your token and its open curve on Monad. Anyone can buy from or sell to the curve at a price that moves with supply; every trade carries a small flat fee. Qualifying buys claim tier seats automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Graduate">
    Your page shows the curve's target. When the community fills it, graduation runs in the same breath: a Uniswap pool is created and seeded, and the pool's liquidity position is **locked forever** in a contract nobody - including us - can withdraw from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep trading, same page">
    After graduation the trade panel on your app page routes to the pool directly. Your chart, your holders, your community - nothing moves off your page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the contracts enforce

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Founder vesting" icon="lock">
    Your allocation sits in a per-launch vesting contract with the cliff and duration you chose at launch. The clock starts **at graduation** - a launch that never graduates vests nothing.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Liquidity locked forever" icon="shield-check">
    The graduated pool's liquidity position is held by a locker contract with no withdrawal path. It cannot be pulled by the founder, the team, or the platform.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Open curve" icon="chart-line">
    No presale, no allowlist on the curve. The same price function for everyone, visible on the page from the first block.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Contribution pool" icon="hand-holding-heart">
    A slice of supply is set aside at launch for you to tip the contributors whose feedback you adopt - recipients don't even need a wallet yet to be owed it.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Who decides what

The mechanics are code; the judgement stays human. Which apps deserve their moment is decided by seat holders and contributors showing up - backing, voting, testing, holding through graduation - not by a formula the platform runs over your community.

<Warning>
  Launching a token is irreversible in the ways that matter: the supply, your founder allocation, and your vesting schedule are fixed at launch. Read the launch screen slowly; the platform shows you every number before you sign.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Not sure you want a token at all? Don't launch one. Community-only apps get the same page, tiers, devlogs, and Discover placement - the token adds a market, not legitimacy.
</Note>
