You built something experimental. FirstFifty's job is to find the fifty people who will care about it as much as you do, and to give both of you the tools to turn that into momentum.

## What is an app on FirstFifty?

An **app** is the unit everything else attaches to. Concretely, it is:

- **A page**: your story, one-liner, links (the website becomes the visitor's "Try it" button), logo, banner, and stage (concept, private alpha, public beta, launched). It lives at a permanent URL you can share anywhere, and it unfurls with a generated card on X and Warpcast.
- **A community with seats**: three tiers open at publish: **Founding 50**, **Early 500**, and unlimited **Public**. Capped seats are numbered in claim order and permanent, which is what makes them worth wanting.
- **A workspace in your dashboard**: devlog, polls, milestones, feedback inbox, member management, team roles, and settings.
- **Optionally, a token**: launched through an open curve on Monad, with founder vesting and locked liquidity enforced by the contracts. An app without a token is a first-class citizen, not a downgrade.

## Why seats work

Scarcity with receipts. A visitor who sees "43 of 50 Founding seats left" understands the offer instantly: show up before the others do, and the platform will remember it, on their profile, on the leaderboards, and (for token apps) on chain as a soulbound proof. Your first fifty stop being an abstraction and become a list of names with numbers next to them.

## Money, plainly

- Publishing an app page is free.
- Launching a token costs a small flat fee, shown at launch time.
- Every launch sets aside a **contribution pool**: a slice of supply you tip to the people whose feedback you adopt. Generosity is built into the mechanics, not bolted on.
