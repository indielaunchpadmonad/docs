A devlog is the heartbeat of building in public: short, dated posts about what actually happened this week. On FirstFifty, each post carries its own visibility, so the inside view becomes a perk your earliest seats actually feel.

## Visibility, per post

| Visibility | Who reads it |
| --- | --- |
| **Public** | Everyone, signed in or not, your marketing surface |
| **Members only** | Anyone holding any seat, including the free Public tier |
| **Specific tier and above** | That tier and every more exclusive tier, e.g. Founding 50 only |

Locked posts show their **title** to everyone, so people below the gate see what they're missing, which is the point of a perk. Gated bodies never leave the server.

## Writing posts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Draft">
    A title and a Markdown body. Say what shipped, what is next, and where you are stuck.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set visibility">
    Public for announcements, members for the working notes, Founding-only for the raw stuff: roadmap doubts, revenue, the honest version.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post">
    Members are notified per their preferences.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What good devlogs look like

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Rhythm beats length" icon="calendar">
    A three-line post every week builds more trust than a monthly essay. Your backers are subscribed to progress, not prose.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Thank your people" icon="user-check">
    Name the tester who found the bug or the Founding seat whose idea shipped. Recognition is the currency here.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Pair a locked post with a public teaser. "Shipped the thing Founding voted for, details inside" converts curiosity into seat claims better than any banner.
</Tip>
