A devlog entry is a short update on what you built, what is next, and where you are stuck. You choose who can read each one.

<Frame caption="A devlog entry on the app page. Visitors see the title and a preview.">
  ![A devlog card on an app page](/images/app-devlog.png)
</Frame>

## Post an entry

Open **Manage**, then the **Devlog** tab. On the left is your **Devlog history**; on the right is the **New entry** form.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give it a title">
    Required, up to 120 characters. For example, "Shipped v0.3 outlining".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the body">
    Markdown, up to 20,000 characters, in a plain text box. Say what shipped, what is next, and what you are stuck on. Quoting real user messages builds trust.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the visibility">
    Pick one of three options:

    | Option | Who can read the body |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Public** | Everyone, signed in or not |
    | **Members only** | Anyone holding any seat, including Public |
    | **Specific tier and above** | The tier you pick, and every tier more exclusive than it |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the tier if you gated it">
    Choosing "Specific tier and above" reveals a **Required tier** picker. A Founding member sees posts gated to Early, but not the other way round.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Post">
    You will see "Devlog posted." Members are notified.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Gated posts still show their **title** and a lock to everyone else, so people see what they are missing. The body itself is never sent to anyone without access.
</Note>

## Change who can read an entry

In **Devlog history**, press **Change visibility** on an entry, choose Public, Members only, or a specific tier, and press **Save**. You will see "Visibility updated." You can also delete an entry with the trash icon and a confirmation.

<Warning>
  You cannot edit the title or body of a posted entry. Check it before you press Post.
</Warning>

## What readers see

<Frame caption="An individual entry has its own permanent page.">
  ![A devlog post page](/images/devlog-post.png)
</Frame>

Readers without access see "This update is for backers", with the tier that unlocks it. Pair a locked post with a public teaser, for example "Shipped the thing Founding voted for, details inside", and it will bring in more seat claims than any banner.
