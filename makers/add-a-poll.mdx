Polls put real decisions to your seat holders. Every member gets one vote, whatever their tier.

<Frame caption="Open polls on an app page, with live percentages and a closing countdown. Tidepool is a demo app made for these docs.">
  ![Polls on an app page](/images/poll-live.png)
</Frame>

## Create a poll

Open **Manage**, then the **Polls** tab. Your polls are on the left and the **New poll** form is on the right.

<Frame caption="The New poll form.">
  ![The New poll form in the Polls tab](/images/poll-composer.png)
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask the question">
    **Question** is up to 200 characters, for example "What should we build next?"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add options">
    Two to eight options, up to 80 characters each. Press **Add option** for more, and use the X to remove one once you have more than two.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the closing time">
    **Closes** defaults to seven days from now. It must be between one hour and thirty days ahead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Create poll">
    You will see "Poll opened." It appears on your app page straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  You can have at most **three open polls** at a time. Wait for one to close before opening another.
</Warning>

## Results and closing

Each option shows a bar with a percentage and the number of voters. A poll closes on its own at its deadline, when the tally is frozen, voters earn Contribution Points (2 each) and everyone is told. Closing a poll early is not available yet.

## What members see

- Seat holders click an option and see "Vote counted". They can tap another option to change their vote.
- Signed-in people without a seat see "join to vote". Signed-out visitors see "sign in to vote".
- After the poll closes, the page shows "voted by 4 of 5 seat holders".

<Tip>
  Ask questions with real consequences: which integration ships first, what to name the next feature. Members who see their vote change the roadmap come back.
</Tip>
