The app page is where a stranger becomes a believer. It should answer three questions in ten seconds: _what is this_, _can I try it_, and _how early am I_.

## Creating the page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Details">
    Name, a 140-character one-liner, category, and stage. Be honest about stage - a concept labelled as launched costs you trust with exactly the people you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Links">
    Website first - **it becomes the "Try it" button at the top of your page**, so point it at whatever a visitor should open: your site, a download, a demo. Then X and GitHub, plus anything else (Farcaster, a Substack).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Story">
    Markdown, up to 5,000 characters. What you're building, why, and what the first fifty get for showing up early. The founder-vesting card on token apps sets the tone worth copying: plain sentences about real mechanics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Logo and banner">
    Square logo, wide banner. They carry your page and your generated share card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Token - or not">
    Choose community-only or token mode. Token mode adds the [launch flow](/makers/token-launch); you can start community-only and it is a deliberate, supported choice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Publishing opens your three tiers automatically and lists you in Discover. Your slug is permanent from this moment - the URL people share never breaks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the live page contains

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Overview tab" icon="house">
    Story, milestones, latest devlog, the tier card with live seats-left counts, polls, and the community forum preview.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Token & chart tab (token apps)" icon="chart-line">
    Live chart, trade panel (curve before graduation, the Uniswap pool after - trading never leaves your page), holders, token facts, and the founder vesting card.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Forum tab" icon="comments">
    Threads and replies from your members. Pin what matters.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Backers page" icon="users">
    Every seat holder with their tier and number, ranked by Contribution Points earned on your app.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Sharing

Every page ships with **Copy link**, **Share on X**, and **Warpcast** buttons, and the link unfurls with your generated card. Backers get their own share moments too - claiming a seat is designed to be posted.

<Tip>
  The single highest-leverage field is the website link. A page with a working "Try it" button converts curious visitors into seat holders; a page without one asks them to believe on faith.
</Tip>
