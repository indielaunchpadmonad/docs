Posting an app takes three short steps: the app, an optional token, and a review. Nothing is public until you publish, and you can save a draft at any point.

<Frame caption="The page you are about to create, as visitors see it. The screenshots in this guide use Tidepool, a demo app made for these docs.">
  ![An app page on FirstFifty](/images/app-hero-v2.png)
</Frame>

## Before you start

- A FirstFifty account. If you need one, see [Create Account](/create-account).
- A name, a one-line pitch, and a website link (or a demo or download link) if you have one.
- Optional: a square logo and a wide banner, as PNG, JPEG or WebP files up to 5 MB each.

## Step 1: App details

Open [firstfifty.xyz/dashboard/apps/new](https://firstfifty.xyz/dashboard/apps/new). The page is titled **Create an app** and shows "Step 1 of 3".

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name your app">
    **App name** is required, 2 to 80 characters. The **URL slug** fills in from the name (lowercase letters, digits and dashes, up to 48). A live hint tells you whether it is free. It is editable until you publish and permanent after.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the one-liner">
    Required, 4 to 140 characters, with a live counter. This is the line under your app's name everywhere.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a category and a stage">
    Categories: AI, Productivity, Finance, Developer tools, Media, Games, Web3, Other. Stages: **Concept** (an idea, nothing shipped yet), **Private alpha** (a handful of invited testers), **Public beta** (open for anyone to try), **Launched** (live and in real use). Be honest about the stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your story">
    Optional, up to 5,000 characters. Use the toolbar (heading, bold, italic, lists, quote, link) or paste Markdown and it is converted for you. Say what you are building, why, and what your first fifty get for showing up early.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a logo and banner">
    Optional. Choose a file for each. They carry your page and your share card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your links">
    Optional: Website, X and GitHub. The **Website link becomes the Try it button** on your page, so point it at whatever a visitor should open.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2: Token (optional)

Choose how the app launches.

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="No token (community-only)" icon="users" horizontal>
    Members get tier badges, devlog access, polls and milestones. You can launch a token later from Manage, and existing members carry over.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Open bonding curve" icon="chart-line" horizontal>
    Launch a token on Monad. You set the token name (up to 32 characters), a ticker (2 to 10 letters or digits), your founder share, vesting terms, and your first buy. One transaction creates the token and makes that buy.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Frame caption="Step 2: community-only or an open bonding curve.">
  ![Step 2 of the app builder: choosing a launch mechanic](/images/post-app-2-token-v2.png)
</Frame>

Other mechanics appear in the list marked "coming soon" and cannot be selected yet. If you are unsure, pick community-only: it is not a permanent choice. See [Token Launch](/makers/token-launch) for what the contracts enforce.

## Step 3: Review and publish

The review screen summarizes everything, with an **Edit** button beside each section. If something is missing, a "Not ready to publish" notice points you to the step to fix.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the summary">
    Confirm the name, slug, story and links. Slugs are permanent once you publish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Press **Publish** for a community-only app, or **Launch $TICKER and publish** for a curve. Publishing opens your three tiers (Founding 50, Early 500, Public) and puts the page in Discover.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your page">
    You will see a message like "Tidepool is live" (with your own app name) and three buttons: **View the live page**, **Open Manage**, and **Back to dashboard**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Choosing **Save as draft** keeps the app private at its slug. Come back from your dashboard, or reopen it with the draft link, and carry on.
</Note>
