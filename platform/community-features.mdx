Every tool on FirstFifty is built to close the loop between a maker and the people most invested in the app. Founding members see updates first, members vote on what gets built, and everyone can watch milestones close.

## Devlog

Each entry has its own permanent URL and a visibility you choose per post.

| Visibility | Who can read it |
| --- | --- |
| Public | Everyone, including visitors without a seat |
| Members | Every seat holder |
| Founding | Only Founding seat holders |

Entries are permanent, so early backers can point to the exact post from when they showed up. The full guide is in [Devlog](/makers/devlog).

## Polls

Attach a poll to a devlog entry or publish it on its own. Seat holders vote, and visitors can see that the poll exists. Good uses are feature priority, naming, UI direction, and release timing. Each vote earns 2 [Contribution Points](/adopters/contribution-points), so a poll gives the maker signal and the voter credit.

## Milestones

Milestones are visible to everyone and listed on the app page. The maker marks them complete as progress lands.

## Replies and feedback

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Threaded replies">
    Members reply directly under a devlog entry, and threads stay organized as the community grows.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Founder pins">
    Makers can pin any reply to the top to surface useful feedback or anchor a follow-up.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Adopted feedback and tips">
    When a maker adopts a piece of feedback, they can tip the person who gave it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Post a Founding-only devlog right after a big milestone. Letting top believers celebrate first is part of what makes a Founding seat worth holding.
</Tip>
