## The basics

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is an app on FirstFifty?">
    An app is a product a maker is building: a tool, game, service, or experiment. On FirstFifty each one gets a page with its story, roadmap, devlog, community, and numbered seats. An app can be a rough concept or a shipped product, and it can stay community-only or also launch a token.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is a seat?">
    A numbered, permanent claim on having been early to one app. Founding has 50 seats, Early has 500, and Public is unlimited. See [Seats & Tiers](/adopters/tiers).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a crypto wallet?">
    Not to join, claim a Public seat, vote, or earn CP. A Monad wallet is needed for token buys, for qualifying buys that claim a capped seat on token apps, and for minting the soulbound backer proof.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## For makers

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to launch a token?">
    No. Community-only is a first-class path. Seats, devlogs, polls, milestones, and the forum work the same with or without a token.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get listed?">
    Every app is reviewed by hand before it appears in Discover. Create a draft from your dashboard at [firstfifty.xyz/dashboard/apps/new](https://firstfifty.xyz/dashboard/apps/new); a complete draft with a story, tiers, and a milestone moves fastest.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much can I allocate to myself?">
    You choose a founder allocation between 0% and 20% of supply. It vests in a contract on a cliff and schedule you set before launch, and cannot be changed once live. See [Token Launch](/makers/token-launch).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens at graduation?">
    When the curve fills, liquidity locks, your vesting clock starts, and token holders sync into your tier feeds. All of it is enforced by the contract.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change things after launch?">
    Operational perks such as office hours or devlog cadence can be updated. Seat caps and contract-level settings cannot.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## For early adopters

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I get a Founding seat?">
    Open an app, check the tier card, and make a single qualifying buy at or above the Founding threshold, or be granted a seat by the maker. Split buys do not add up. If Founding is full, you roll down to the next tier with room.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What do I earn for being early?">
    A permanent seat number, Contribution Points, a place on the leaderboards, and an optional soulbound backer proof minted to your wallet. See [Contribution Points](/adopters/contribution-points).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="If I sell my tokens, do I lose my seat?">
    No. A seat records that you were early. It is not a balance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When do leaderboard scores appear?">
    All four boards are live. You appear on Top Believers as soon as your belief score is above zero, and a graduation triples the value of your seats and early buys. See [Leaderboards](/adopters/leaderboards).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Founding and Early?">
    |  | Founding | Early |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Seats per app | 50 | 500 |
    | Content | Founding, Members, and Public | Members and Public |
    | Seat CP on claim | 100 minus seat number | 100 minus seat number |
    | Buy threshold | Higher | Lower |
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
