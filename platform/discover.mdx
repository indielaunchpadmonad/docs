The discover feed is intentionally short. Every app on it has been reviewed and approved by the FirstFifty team, so you are browsing real makers who are actively building, not an endless scroll of abandoned listings.

## Browsing the feed

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Stage" icon="layer-group">
    Find apps that are just starting versus those close to a token graduation.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Token" icon="coins">
    Separate apps planning a Monad token launch from community-only apps.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Newest" icon="arrow-up-wide-short">
    Sort by recently approved to catch new arrivals first.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Categories" icon="tags">
    All, AI, Productivity, Finance, Developer Tools, Media, Games, Web3, or Other.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What each listing shows

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Identity">
    App name, hero image, and category, so you can tell at a glance whether it is for you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Seat availability">
    How many Founding and Early seats are still open. Once they are gone, they are gone.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Token status">
    Whether the app is community-only, planning a token, or already live on its curve.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Latest devlog">
    A preview of the most recent public devlog, so you can see what the maker shipped lately.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Claiming a seat from the feed

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find an app">
    Open [firstfifty.xyz/discover](https://firstfifty.xyz/discover) and narrow with the filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read its page">
    Check the story, milestones, and public devlog to see how the maker builds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the seats">
    The tier cards show live seat counts and what each tier needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim yours">
    Join or make a qualifying buy for the tier you want. Your seat number is assigned on the spot and stays yours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The Closest to Graduation board on the [leaderboards](/adopters/leaderboards) is a good shortcut to apps that are about to hit a milestone.
</Tip>
