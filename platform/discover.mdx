The FirstFifty discover feed is intentionally short and curated — not an endless scroll. Every app that appears on it has been onboarded by hand, which means you're browsing a tight list of indie makers who are actively building and genuinely looking for early believers. There are no algorithm-stuffed listings here, no paid placements, and no abandoned projects left to collect dust. What you see is what's actually shipping.

## Browsing the feed

Four controls sit at the top of the feed. Use them in any combination to narrow down to projects that match what you're looking for.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Stage" icon="layer-group" href="/platform/discover">
    Filter apps by their current launch stage — useful for finding projects that are just getting started versus those close to token graduation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Token" icon="coins" href="/platform/discover">
    Toggle between apps planning a token launch on Monad and community-only apps that have opted out of the token mechanic entirely.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sort: Newest" icon="arrow-up-wide-short" href="/platform/discover">
    Sort the feed by the most recently onboarded apps so you catch new arrivals before everyone else does.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Categories" icon="tags" href="/platform/discover">
    Filter by: **All**, **AI**, **Productivity**, **Finance**, **Developer Tools**, **Media**, **Games**, **Web3**, or **Other**.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What each app listing shows

Every card in the discover feed gives you enough signal to decide whether to click through — without requiring a full page visit for every project.

- **App name and hero image** — the maker's chosen identity for the project at a glance.
- **Category** — one of the nine category tags so you can quickly confirm fit.
- **Tier availability** — how many Founding 50 and Early 500 slots are still open. Once they're gone, they're gone.
- **Token status** — whether the app is planning a Monad token launch, already live on the bonding curve, or community-only.
- **Devlog preview** — a snippet of the maker's most recent public devlog entry, so you can see what they've shipped lately.

## Claiming a slot from the feed

Slots are first-come, first-served. The sooner you move, the lower your slot number — and that number is permanent.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a project">
    Browse the discover feed at [firstfifty.xyz/discover](https://firstfifty.xyz/discover). Use the stage, token, and category filters to surface apps that match your interests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open its app page">
    Click the listing to open the full app page. Read the maker's story, browse their milestones, and scroll the public devlog to get a feel for how they build.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check tier availability">
    Look at the tier section on the app page to confirm Founding 50 or Early 500 slots are still available. The live count updates in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim your slot">
    Hit the claim button for the tier you want. Your slot number is assigned immediately and recorded permanently on your profile and the app's member list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Sort by **Closest to Graduation** on the [leaderboard](/adopters/leaderboards) to find apps that are nearly at their token graduation milestone — the point where the bonding curve fills, liquidity locks, and retroactive allocations vest.
</Tip>
