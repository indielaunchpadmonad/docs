Your FirstFifty account is the root of your identity here: your seats, Contribution Points, badges, and posts all hang off it. A wallet is optional and comes later, if at all.

<Frame caption="The sign in page. It creates accounts too.">
  ![The FirstFifty sign in page](/images/login.png)
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Go to [firstfifty.xyz/login](https://firstfifty.xyz/login) and sign up with **email + password** or **Google**. One door does both: the login page creates accounts too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    We send a confirmation link. Verifying unlocks notifications for seat claims, devlog drops from apps you back, tips you receive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a handle">
    Your handle is how makers and other backers see you, and it gives you a public profile page at <code>firstfifty.xyz/profile/you</code>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a wallet, only when you need one">
    Everything social works without a wallet. Connect one (the button lives in the top bar once you're signed in) when you want to buy a token, claim a seat with a buy, or mint your backer proof. The wallet attaches to your account, so your on-chain history and your profile are one identity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What needs a wallet, what doesn't

| Works with just an account | Needs a connected wallet |
| --- | --- |
| Browsing Discover, backing community-only apps | Buying or selling an app's token |
| Reading devlogs, voting in polls, posting in forums | Claiming a seat through a qualifying buy |
| Giving feedback, earning Contribution Points | Minting your soulbound backer proof |
| Receiving tips (they wait for you) | Claiming tips out to your wallet |

<Tip>
  Tips you earn for adopted feedback are held for you even without a wallet. Link one later and claim them whenever you like.
</Tip>
