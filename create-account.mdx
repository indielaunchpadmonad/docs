Creating a FirstFifty account takes less than two minutes and unlocks both sides of the platform — backing indie apps as an early adopter or publishing your own app page as a maker. Early adopters can sign up and start claiming tier slots immediately. Makers enter a short onboarding flow and the team will reach out about going live once your draft is ready.

## Sign up for a new account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the sign-up page">
    Navigate to [firstfifty.xyz/signup](https://firstfifty.xyz/signup) in your browser. The sign-up page is available to anyone — no invite code required for early adopters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email and create a password">
    Type in your email address and choose a strong password. Google sign-in is coming soon — once it's enabled, you'll see a "Continue with Google" button on the same page as a one-click alternative.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your email address">
    Check your inbox for a confirmation email from FirstFifty and click the verification link. If it doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder. The link expires after 24 hours — if it expires, return to the sign-up page and request a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your path">
    After confirming your email, you'll land on a short onboarding screen. Select **Browse apps** to jump straight into the discovery feed as an early adopter, or select **Start a draft** to begin building your maker app page. You can always switch roles later from your account settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sign in to an existing account

If you already have a FirstFifty account, go to [firstfifty.xyz/login](https://firstfifty.xyz/login) and enter your email and password. After a successful sign-in, you'll land on your dashboard — the discovery feed if you're primarily an adopter, or your app drafts and live pages if you're a maker.

## Reset your password

Forgot your password? Go to [firstfifty.xyz/forgot-password](https://firstfifty.xyz/forgot-password) and enter the email address associated with your account. FirstFifty will send you a password reset link. Click the link in that email, enter your new password, and confirm it. The reset link is valid for one hour. If it expires before you use it, simply visit the forgot-password page again and request a fresh link.

<Note>
  Maker accounts are currently onboarded by hand during early access. After you create an account and start a draft, the FirstFifty team will review your app page and reach out about going live. If you're not ready to draft yet, you can drop your email on the [homepage](https://firstfifty.xyz) to join the maker waitlist and we'll contact you as spots open.
</Note>
